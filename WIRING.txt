PHARMAVAULT - FINAL INDEPENDENT-STATION WIRING
Reviewed 25 November 2026

Power OFF every board before changing wires. Use printed GPIO/IO numbers,
not physical header positions. PBesp=ESP32-S3; ESPmsp=original FireBeetle V1.0.

A. PATIENT PILLBOX - PBesp ESP32-S3

STEPPER (SIGNAL PINS UNCHANGED)
  ULN2003 IN1 -> PBesp GPIO5.
  ULN2003 IN2 -> PBesp GPIO6.
  ULN2003 IN3 -> PBesp GPIO7.
  ULN2003 IN4 -> PBesp GPIO8.
  28BYJ-48 motor plug -> ULN2003 white motor socket.

MOTOR POWER
  Independent pillbox: use a verified pillbox-local 5 V rail for ULN2003 +.
  Connect ULN2003 - to the local supply ground and the S3 ground.
  Use S3 USB 5V/VBUS only if it supports the motor while Wi-Fi remains stable.
  Bench arrangement with MSP Booster power: ULN2003 + to H2.21 (5 V),
  ULN2003 - to Booster GND, and S3 GND to that same ground. Keep positive
  supplies separate. This bench arrangement physically links the stations.

IR HAND SENSOR - UNCHANGED
  VCC -> PBesp3V3; GND -> PBespGND; OUT -> PBesp GPIO14.
  Keep the existing3.3V-compatible module and activeLOW operation.

THERMISTOR - MOVE THE WHOLE EXISTING DIVIDER
  Remove its junction connection from MSP P7.0/A12/H2.28.
  Remove its supply/ground connections from the MSP430.
  PBesp GPIO13 (driven HIGH = 3.3 V by firmware) -> existing fixed10k resistor -> junction.
  Junction -> PBesp GPIO1 (ADC1_CH0).
  Junction -> existing10k NTC, Beta3950 -> PBespGND.
  The NTC is the lower leg towardGND; fixed10k is the upper leg toward3V3.
  BoosterJ2 can remain open but is no longer in this measurement circuit.

DHT11 HUMIDITY MODULE - MOVE ALL THREE CONNECTIONS
  Disconnect DATA from MSP P1.5/H2.38 -> connect DATA to PBesp GPIO2.
  Disconnect its old MSP power/GND -> connect VCC/3.3 to PBesp3V3,
  and moduleGND to PBespGND.
  Follow your module's labelled DATA/3.3/GND positions. No extra divider is
  used on DATA. Primary temperature is NTC; DHT temperature is diagnostic.

PBesp GPIO3,4,17 remain UNUSED in wireless mode. GPIO13 supplies the NTC divider. No UART wire goes to the
MSP430. No SG90/SG92R/HC-SR04 or old ultrasonic divider is used.

B. HEALTHCARE STATION - MSP430 + ESPmsp FIREBEETLE

LOCAL UART - THESE THREE WIRES STAY WITH THE HEALTHCARE STATION
  MSP P3.3 / BoosterH2.4 TX -> FireBeetle D11 / GPIO16 RX.
  FireBeetle D10 / GPIO17 TX -> MSP P3.4 / BoosterH2.3 RX.
  MSPGND / H2.20 orH2.22 -> FireBeetleGND.
  UART9600baud8N1,3.3V logic. No level shifter is needed for this local UART.
  Do NOT use FireBeetle's printed TX/RX pins (GPIO1/3), which serve USB serial.
  Power MSP430 and FireBeetle through their own USB cables. Do not connect
  their positive5V/VCC or3V3 rails together.

NFC RC522 - ORIGINAL MSP CONNECTIONS RETAINED
  VCC -> MSP target3V3; GND -> MSPGND; IRQ left unconnected.
  MOSI -> P3.0 / H2.15.
  MISO -> P3.1 / H2.14.
  SCK -> P3.2 / H2.7.
  SDA/SS -> P2.3 / H2.12 (SPI chip select, not I2C).
  RST -> P1.6 / H2.5.
  LCD -> existing fitted BoosterH3 connection; LEDs/power jumpers unchanged.

C. REMOVE THE OLD BETWEEN-STATION WIRES
  Remove MSP P3.3 -> S3GPIO4 and S3GPIO17 -> MSP P3.4.
  After moving motor power and all pillbox sensors locally as above, remove
  the MSP-to-S3 GND jumper too. Do NOT remove the local MSP-to-FireBeetle GND.
  For the independent arrangement there are no inter-station wires. Keep a
  common MSP/S3 ground for as long as the Booster powers the pillbox motor.
