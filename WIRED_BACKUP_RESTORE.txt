RESTORE THE ORIGINAL WIRED VERSION

04_WIRED_BACKUP is the prior RepeatTimer package preserved byte-for-byte.
It uses the ORIGINAL architecture: NTC/DHT11 onMSP430 and a directUART toS3.
Returning to it requires firmware AND wiring changes, not just a sketch swap.

1. Stop/pause operation. Power off every board. Disconnect FireBeetleUART
   wires from MSP P3.3/P3.4; the FireBeetle can be unplugged.
2. Restore MSPP3.3/H2.4 TX -> S3GPIO4 RX.
   Restore S3GPIO17 TX -> MSPP3.4/H2.3 RX. Restore MSPGND -> S3GND.
3. Move the NTC divider supply/ground back to MSP3V3/GND. Move its junction
   from S3GPIO1 back to MSPP7.0/A12/H2.28. Fixed10k upper/NTC lower remains;
   BoosterJ2 must beOPEN.
4. Move DHT DATA from S3GPIO2 back to MSPP1.5/H2.38, and its VCC/GND toMSP.
5. Restore the original ULN2003 power from BoosterH2.21/5V and GND, retaining
   the MSP-S3ground reference. MotorIN1..4 remain S3GPIO5/6/7/8; IR remains14.
   Never join the two boards' positive5V rails together.
6. Upload backup ESP32/PharmaVault_ESP32S3/PharmaVault_ESP32S3.ino toS3.
7. Load backup CCS/PharmaVault_MSP430/Firmware/PharmaVault_MSP430.out toMSP,
   or build its original CCSproject. Resume/Run. Do not use healthcareMSPcode
   with this old sensor arrangement.
8. Expected banners: PHARMAVAULT repeat timer test 1 and
   PHARMAVAULT MSP430 ready (weekly 1; UART fix 1).
9. Ctrl+F5 dashboard; verify readings/card, restore clock/alignment and enable.

Both S3versions share the daily history/settings format. Do not erase flash
to bypass lockouts. Wireless refillinstruction metadata is ignored by the old
wired version and remains stored for a later switch back to wireless.
To return to independent wireless, follow rootWIRING.txt and upload the three
rootlisted programs. Do not attach both FireBeetleTX and S3TX toMSPRX together.
