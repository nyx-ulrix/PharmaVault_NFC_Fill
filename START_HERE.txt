PHARMAVAULT - NFC DISPENSING AND FILL-HEIGHT PERCENTAGE

WHAT CHANGED
When tap dispensing is enabled, a recognized card assigned to a calibrated
compartment commands this sequence:
  SG90 closes -> SG92R positions that compartment -> SG90 opens -> SG90 closes.
The motors receive PWM one at a time. The existing manual controls remain.
Ultrasonic distance now produces an estimated fill-height percentage after
you capture an empty reference (0%) and the intended full reference (100%).
No additional components, wires, physical buttons or switches are required.

UPDATE BOTH BOARDS
ESP32:
  Open ESP32/PharmaVault_ESP32S3/PharmaVault_ESP32S3.ino in your working
  Arduino IDE. Use ESP32S3 Dev Module, Arduino-ESP32 core3.x, your existing
  working port/USB settings. Upload without erasing all flash.
  No additional Arduino libraries or source edits are required.
  Serial Monitor115200, send ?; expected:
    PHARMAVAULT NFC + fill firmware 1

MSP430:
  The simplest route is to load the included, precompiled
    CCS/PharmaVault_MSP430/Firmware/PharmaVault_MSP430.out
  through your existing CCS debug target, then Resume/Run.
  To rebuild instead, replace main.c, rc522.c AND rc522.h in your current
  project with the included versions. Build, Debug/load, then Resume/Run.
  Do not add second copies of these source files.
  MSP debug console9600, expected startup marker:
    PHARMAVAULT MSP430 ready (NFC sequence 1; UART fix 1)
  Project import details are in CCS/PharmaVault_MSP430/README.txt.

The old MSP firmware cannot provide the removal/heartbeat events required
for the new automatic mode. Temperature/humidity/UART pins stay unchanged.

DASHBOARD
Join your existing PharmaVault Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1/.
Username demo; use the existing password printed by ? on ESP serial.
Refresh with Ctrl+F5. Existing Wi-Fi credentials, NFC assignments, flap
positions and carousel positions remain saved. Tap dispensing starts PAUSED
after every ESP restart or MSP reader restart/reconnect.

NFC SETUP - KEEP TAP DISPENSING PAUSED
1. In Flap positions - SG90, test and save the actual open/closed positions
   with the final linkage. Previously saved verified positions are retained.
2. In Carousel positions - SG92R, test/save each reachable compartment's
   alignment. Use real measured positions, not invented evenly spaced angles.
   Previously saved positions are retained.
3. Tap a card while mode is paused. In Assign NFC tags to compartments,
   select its slot, Use last scanned tag, then Save assignment.
4. In the NFC section, confirm the mechanism/calibration checks and click
   Enable tap dispensing. This enables the mode; it does not move a motor.
5. Remove all cards. Wait for the reader-clear/Ready message (up to about5s
   if the reader was already empty). Then tap the assigned card.

WHAT A TAP DOES
The selected compartment must have a saved carousel position and the flap
must have saved distinct open/closed positions. An accepted tap commands:
  GPIO3 SG90 closed650ms
  detach GPIO3 -> GPIO5 SG92R saved compartment position1000ms
  detach GPIO5 -> GPIO3 SG90 open650ms, then closed650ms
  detach GPIO3. Total commanded motion is about2.95 seconds.
The final log records that PWM was sent, not proof that medicine emerged.

Holding a card in the reader does not intentionally repeat the cycle. Remove
it for at least one second, wait at least three seconds after the previous
motion finishes, then retap for another cycle. Busy/cooldown requests are
rejected, not saved as a future cycle. A new tap waiting over about500ms
before its first UART transmission expires and must be repeated.
UART retries reuse their sequence and cannot start duplicate cycles.
Unassigned cards and slots without calibrated positions do not actuate.

Pause tap dispensing before registering cards, changing dummy contents or
calibrating. Accepted manual movement/calibration/tag edits also pause it.
After restart/reconnect/PWM fault, inspect the mechanism before re-enabling.
Pause mode prevents later taps; it is not an emergency stop and does not
interrupt a motor sequence already executing. HTTP/dashboard refreshes may
pause during the sequence, while the MCU UART continues to be serviced.

FILL-HEIGHT PERCENTAGE
Mount the HC-SR04 above the viewed compartment, aimed at its bottom and the
top surface of the contents. Keep it fixed while capturing these references:
1. Pause tap dispensing.
2. Empty the viewed compartment. Wait for Ready to capture, confirm, then
   click Capture 0% empty. This saves distance E.
3. Fill to the intended maximum level with representative dummy contents.
   Wait for steady readings, confirm, then Capture 100% full. This saves F.
4. The display estimates:
     fill percent = 100 * (E - measured distance) / (E - F)
   Example E150mm, F50mm: 150mm=0%, 125mm=25%, 100mm=50%, 50mm=100%.

The full surface must remain at least20mm from the sensor. E-F must be at
least20mm; empty distance must therefore be at least40mm. Use more clearance
if the actual mounted setup needs it to produce repeatable echoes.
The estimate uses the median of five consecutive valid distance readings,
spread<=10mm and latest age<=2s. Missing/stale/unstable echoes or motor motion
make it unavailable. Small deviations up to10mm beyond a reference clamp
to0%/100%; distances farther outside that band are unavailable.

This estimates the height of the reflecting contents surface, not pill
count, remaining doses or exact volume. Uneven piles, gaps and narrow walls
can give misleading returns. Test repeatability in the actual dispenser.
It measures the current view only. It does not store an inventory reading
for all eight compartments. A common calibration is suitable only when the
viewed compartments have matching geometry and full/empty reference heights.
The software cannot verify which compartment is physically under the sensor.

The previous arbitrary 'loaded' reference is deliberately NOT converted to
100% full. Capture both new references once. They then persist across restart.
Recalibrate after moving the sensor or changing the compartment geometry.

UNCHANGED WIRING
  SG90 flap signal GPIO3; SG92R carousel signal GPIO5.
  MSP P3.3 TX -> ESP GPIO4 RX; ESP GPIO17 TX -> MSP P3.4 RX; link9600 8N1.
  HC-SR04 TRIG GPIO13; VCC confirmed5V; GND common with both MCUs.
  HC ECHO -> existing2.2k -> GPIO14 junction -> existing3.2k -> commonGND.
  Keep all grounds common. Keep the independently working servo5V supply
  arrangements; do not join different USB sources' positive5V rails together.
  NTC, DHT11 and RC522 retain their existing3.3V connections.

Two connected servos still share the limits of their USB source and wiring.
Sequential PWM reduces simultaneous commanded motion but does not prove
adequate power under load. A command-completed message is not motor feedback.
No schedules, per-dose lockout, phone/cloud alerts or verified intake are
implemented. Each accepted fresh tap requests another cycle; demonstrate
with dummy contents and only mechanically reachable carousel positions.
