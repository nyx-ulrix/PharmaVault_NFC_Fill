PHARMAVAULT - INDEPENDENT PATIENT PILLBOX + HEALTHCARE STATION
17 November 2026

FINAL ARRANGEMENT
PBesp = ESP32-S3 in the patient's pillbox. It owns the IR hand sensor, NTC,
DHT11, ULN2003/28BYJ-48, dashboard, saved schedule and seven-day dose journal.
ESPmsp = original FireBeetle ESP32-E V1.0 beside the healthcare MSP430.
It is the wireless-to-UART bridge. MSP430 owns RC522 NFC, LCD and Booster LEDs.

  [Patient pillbox: sensors + motor + PBesp]
                  <--- protected Wi-Fi --->
  [ESPmsp FireBeetle <-- local UART --> MSP430 + NFC reader + LCD]

The healthcare laptop joins PBesp's PharmaVault Wi-Fi and opens its dashboard.
No signal, 5V, 3V3 or ground jumper is required between the two stations after
the motor power and environmental sensors are moved as WIRING.txt describes.
Each station still needs USB power. Wireless does not add a battery/charger.
For a bench demo both stations may use laptop USB ports; there are no direct
GPIO/power jumpers between their PCBs. The Wi-Fi link needs no external router.

FIRST INSTALL
1. Extract the WHOLE ZIP. On a fresh PC, follow FRESH_PC_INSTALL.txt.
2. Power off all boards, then follow WIRING.txt. The old thermistor/DHT11 and
   ULN2003 power connections to the MSP430 MUST move to the pillbox S3.
3. Upload the three matching programs above. Do not erase the existing S3's
   flash: that preserves credentials, daily times, pack dates and dose history.
4. MSP430: load/debug, then Resume/Run. Do not leave it stopped at main.
5. Pair ESPmsp once using the procedure below.
6. Join PharmaVault Wi-Fi on the laptop, open http://pharmavault (or http://192.168.4.1), Ctrl+F5.
7. In Refill, enter the Day1..Day7 refill instructions and Save instructions.
8. Run TEST_AND_DEMO.txt to verify readings, card lookup, warnings and motion.

PAIR ESPmsp TO PBesp ONCE
1. Open PBesp's Serial Monitor at115200 baud. Send ?.
   Expected banner: PHARMAVAULT PBesp wireless 2 (local sensors)
2. Copy its 'Wi-Fi name' (PharmaVault-...) and 'Wi-Fi password' (PharmaVault67).
3. Select the FireBeetle's COM port. Serial Monitor115200, line ending Newline.
   Expected banner: PHARMAVAULT wireless bridge 1
4. Send SETUP. Enter the exact PBesp Wi-Fi name, then its exact Wi-Fi password
   at the two prompts. No source-code credentials need editing.
5. Expect 'Pair saved' and 'BRIDGE ONLINE'. Pairing is saved in the FireBeetle
   and reconnects automatically. Send ? there to see connection/frame counters.
6. Dashboard username: demo; password: PharmaVault67 (same as the Wi-Fi).
   The dashboard should say the healthcare station is connected.
   Windows may report No Internet on this access point; stay connected.
   Use automatic DHCP for the laptop. Do not assign it192.168.4.1: that is
   PBesp's address, not the laptop's. Open http://, not https://.
7. The MSP430 serial console remains9600baud. Its new banner is:
   PHARMAVAULT healthcare MSP430 ready (wireless provider 1)

WHAT NFC DOES
Attach a compatible kit card/tag physically to this pillbox. Bring the RC522
reader to it, or bring the box/tag near the reader. This build handles ONE
wirelessly paired pillbox. A scan opens information from that paired PBesp;
UIDs do not automatically select among several unrelated boxes.
The MSP receives weekly status, fetches Days1..7 refill instructions, then
pages through them on its16x2LCD. Instructions are up to48 printable ASCII
characters per day, shown in16-character segments. A blank day shows Not set.
No medicine names or doses are invented. Enter the intended instructions.
Retap after editing instructions to fetch the new copy. A tap never dispenses,
resets the week, confirms refill or changes the daily timer.

MONITORING
PBesp reads local NTC temperature and DHT11 humidity approximately every5s,
deferring reads during motor motion/active hand timing. It sends readings,
sensor/environment warnings, missed/overdue and refill status to the provider.
The MSP LCD alternates environment/box status when not showing refill pages.
New warnings interrupt the guide; a repeated unchanged warning does not
continuously restart it. The dashboard contains the full dated dose history.
After about16s without fresh provider messages, the MSP marks data stale and
does not present old temperatures as current. The FireBeetle reconnects by
itself. PBesp's local sensing/scheduling continues if the provider is off.

NORMAL USE AND TESTS
The daily time ARMS that day's compartment; a fresh hand held1.5s moves the
stepper once. IR is activeLOW; no website calibration is needed. Seven medicine
compartments keep their assigned dates, with slot8 empty parking. Refill is
confirmed for the whole week, not each day. NFC is not required for dispensing.
The normal weekly timer and the repeatable10s READY/30s OVERDUE test remain.
Test overdue is explicitly labelled TEST on the dashboard and provider LCD.
There is no ultrasonic fill sensor or SG90/SG92R in this release.

After PBesp upload/restart, restore the real clock and actual alignment, then
explicitly enable normal operation. The saved timer is retained; do not start
a new pack merely because the PC or firmware changed. NORMAL_OPERATION.txt
describes missed-day handling and retained daily lockouts.

BACKUP AND LIMITS
WIRED_BACKUP_RESTORE.txt restores the original firmware AND original sensor/
power wiring. Do not mix its older MSP program with this new PBesp layout.
Overdue alerts are local dashboard/LCD notices, not email/SMS/cloud pushes.
Completion means commanded steps finished; pill delivery is not measured.
VALIDATION.txt distinguishes native MSP build, host tests and unverified
ESP32 target compilation/physical operation. All required sources are included.
