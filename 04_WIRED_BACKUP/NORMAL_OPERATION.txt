PHARMAVAULT WEEKLY OPERATION / RECOVERY

DATE-SPECIFIC COMPARTMENTS
The first calendar date displayed for a new pack uses compartment 1, the
following date compartment 2, through compartment 7. Compartment 8 is empty.
The calendar continues while paused/off; seven days means seven scheduled
dates, not seven successful dispensing cycles. The times use Singapore UTC+8.
Changing the timer does not change the pack's dates or erase daily lockouts.

The daily time ARMS availability. It does not rotate the carousel by itself.
Fresh hand detection triggers one forward compartment movement. A clear
sensor for 1 second is required first; the LOW input must then remain steady
for 1500 ms. Keeping the hand there cannot trigger repeated cycles. Digital
IR sensitivity is adjusted physically, not by website calibration.

At the alert time, uncompleted prepared dates are flagged overdue. Late
dispensing is permitted until midnight. After midnight an uncompleted date
becomes MISSED and cannot be automatically retried. Missing past dates are
also recorded when an off/paused unit resumes with a valid clock.

MISSED DAY EXAMPLE
Day 1 was dispensed: physical position is compartment 1.
Day 2 was missed: its medicine is still in compartment 2.
On day 3, the software targets compartment 3; it does not dispense slot 2.
It blocks motor movement because physical position 1 is not predecessor 2.
A worker pauses, physically removes day 2 medicine, manually positions empty
slot 2 at the outlet, selects actual compartment 2 under alignment, confirms
the position and missed-dose handling, and enables the schedule.
Day 3's one hand presentation can then advance to compartment 3.
Never rotate loaded missed doses through the release outlet.
The system cannot sense whether the worker performed the physical steps.

WEEKLY REFILL
The reminder appears when day 7's dispense command completes, or at midnight
after day 7 when not completed. A missed day still counts. Day 7 may be
dispensed late until midnight; an overdue alert alone does not cancel it.
The reminder is saved and remains after restart or clock rollback.
No day 8 normal dose is armed. Normal operation pauses until the whole pack
is refilled and empty parking compartment 8 is confirmed on the website.
Any uncollected contents must be removed before moving/refilling the carousel.

The refill button is available only for first setup or a finished pack,
with valid clock, idle/cooldown motor, verified stepper, working settings
storage and IR test mode off. Both physical refill confirmations are required.
It does not move the motor or erase earlier daily attempts/history.
If today's date already has a record, the new pack starts tomorrow; otherwise
it starts today. Existing daily and alert times are unchanged. The schedule
is enabled automatically after a successful whole-week confirmation.

NFC
Tap any supported kit card to display the seven-day status for 8 seconds:
  Initial setup: Load 7 days / Confirm in web
  Active: Week day N of 7 / Timer unchanged
  Before first date: Week starts soon / Timer unchanged
  Refill due: Refill all 7days / Confirm in web
  Clock not restored: Set clock in web / Timer saved
The missing space in "7days" keeps the first LCD line within 16 characters.
NFC never dispenses, changes a medicine assignment or confirms a new refill.
NTC/DHT readings and warnings resume on the LCD afterward. Reader restart
does not pause the ESP32's daily mode because NFC is now optional.

RESTART / MAINTENANCE
ESP32 power-up: coils stay off; schedule and test mode start paused; the
physical position is unknown and clock needs sync. Saved times/weekly pack
and journal remain. Restore the correct clock, inspect for missed doses,
confirm actual position and enable. Do not start a new pack midweek.
If today already completed, confirm today's compartment. Before today's
attempt, confirm its predecessor (N-1, or 8 before day 1).

An interrupted/failed attempt is faulted after restart and cannot auto-retry.
The journal is saved BEFORE starting the motor. A "Completed" record means
the commanded steps finished, not that pill delivery or ingestion was sensed.
Correct clock rollback warnings; old calendar dates cannot be replayed.

Changing stepper settings, jogging or entering IR test mode cancels any
pending READY record as Fault. This prevents mechanical maintenance from
leaving a stale daily dose resumable. Align-only confirmation preserves a
READY day but must match its predecessor. Changing schedule/Wi-Fi/clock
pauses normal operation and does not reset recorded attempts.
Pause/Stop prevents a new rotation; a bounded rotation already running may
finish before a dashboard request is serviced. It is not an emergency stop.

TEST MODE
Empty/dummy contents only. It needs verified steps/direction but no clock,
card or weekly refill. A fresh 1.5-second hand hold advances one compartment
each time after the clear/cooldown sequence. Counts are test commands, not
doses. Closing the browser does not stop test mode. Stop explicitly; reboot
also stops it. After test movement, inspect and confirm alignment before
resuming the date-specific daily pack. Test mode cannot reset a daily lockout.

UPGRADE
This release retains credentials, stepper settings, daily times and old
history. Earlier firmware pending READY/uncertain records become Fault so
they cannot be resumed under the new compartment semantics. A new seven-day
pack confirmation is needed once; the software cannot infer existing pills.
The daily-journal version changes. Do not downgrade firmware or erase flash
to bypass a lockout. Older versions reject the weekly journal.
