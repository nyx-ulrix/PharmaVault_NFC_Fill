PHARMAVAULT - SEVEN-DAY PACK
Weekly firmware 1 - 13 November 2026

WHAT CHANGED
Set the daily and overdue times once. They remain saved until you edit them.
There is no daily medicine-name entry, NFC tap or next-compartment refill.
Compartments 1-7 hold seven DIFFERENT, date-specific daily doses.
Compartment 8 stays empty and is the starting/parking position.
At the daily time, today's assigned compartment becomes available. The motor
does not move until the IR sensor sees a fresh hand for 1.5 seconds.
One scheduled attempt per calendar date; an uncertain attempt is not retried.
NFC is optional: any supported kit card shows weekly status on the MSP LCD.
After day 7's dispense command completes, or at midnight after day 7 if it
was missed, the dashboard asks the worker to REFILL ALL 7 DAYS.

UPDATE BOTH BOARDS; NO WIRING CHANGES
1. ESP32: open ESP32/PharmaVault_ESP32S3/PharmaVault_ESP32S3.ino in Arduino IDE.
   Keep your working ESP32S3 Dev Module / COM / USB settings and core 3.x.
   No external Arduino libraries are required. Verify, then upload.
   Do not erase all flash: this keeps Wi-Fi credentials, the saved timer,
   stepper settings and previous daily lockouts/history.
   Serial monitor 115200: send ? and check "PHARMAVAULT weekly firmware 1".
2. MSP430: import/build CCS/PharmaVault_MSP430 as ONE CCS project, or load
   its supplied Firmware/PharmaVault_MSP430.out using your working debugger.
   Do not add the ImportAlternative project on top of the normal project.
   Start/Resume the target. Console 9600 should show:
   PHARMAVAULT MSP430 ready (weekly 1; UART fix 1)
   CCS/PharmaVault_MSP430/README.txt gives the detailed import instructions.
3. Join the existing PharmaVault Wi-Fi. Open http://192.168.4.1, or the
   same-Wi-Fi address printed by the ESP32. Username demo; password printed
   by ?. Refresh with Ctrl+F5. The page must show the seven-day pack panel.

ONE-TIME WEEKLY SETUP
4. Pause operation and remove previous contents. Confirm the motor direction
   and saved steps per compartment using an EMPTY carousel if needed.
   Previously verified stepper settings are retained; do not re-save them
   during an active daily dose unnecessarily.
5. Sync the clock from the laptop; check its Singapore date/time.
6. Check the daily time and the later overdue-alert time, for example 13:00
   and 14:00. Save only if changing them. They repeat without daily edits.
7. Physically park EMPTY compartment 8 at the outlet/start reference so the
   next forward one-compartment movement presents compartment 1. Remove
   missed/old pills first. Load the correct doses for days 1-7 in compartments
   1-7. Nothing should be dispensed merely by loading or parking the mechanism.
8. In the weekly panel, tick BOTH confirmations (seven days filled, empty
   compartment 8 physically parked). Click the refill/start-week button.
   This records the pack, confirms position 8 and enables the saved schedule.
   It sends no motor movement. Check the displayed week start date.
   If any daily record already exists for today, the new pack starts TOMORROW
   to preserve the one-attempt-per-date lockout. Otherwise it starts today.
   Confirming after today's daily time can make day 1 available immediately;
   it still requires a new hand presentation to move.

EVERYDAY USE
9. At/after the daily time, clear the IR area for at least 1 second, then hold
   a hand near it continuously for 1.5 seconds. The carousel advances ONE
   compartment and the date's record becomes Completed when commands finish.
   Remove the hand afterward. Repeated presentations cannot repeat that date.
   No card tap, daily refill or daily timer setting is needed.
10. If not completed by the alert time, an overdue alert appears in this
    local dashboard. A late attempt is allowed until midnight. The alert is
    a webpage status, not a phone push/email/cloud notification.
11. At the weekly refill reminder, remove remaining old/missed doses, park
    at EMPTY 8 and refill all seven days. Confirm the two weekly checkboxes
    again. The SAME daily/alert times remain selected.

IF A DAY IS MISSED
Each compartment remains assigned to its date. The next day must not receive
yesterday's medicine. Rotation is blocked if today's compartment is not next.
Pause; remove all missed doses before manually realigning the carousel.
For day N, position the outlet at compartment N-1 (8 for day 1), confirm
that actual position and the missed-dose handling checkbox, then re-enable.
Alignment confirmation itself sends no motor movement. Do not motor-jog
through loaded compartments. Never mark a position you have not checked.
Days missed while off/paused are recorded as missed when the clock is restored.

AFTER AN ESP32 RESTART
The timer, weekly pack, refill reminder and daily attempts remain saved.
The board has no battery-backed clock or position sensor: sync the clock,
inspect missed doses, confirm actual alignment and explicitly enable again.
Do NOT confirm a new refill merely to resume the same pack. No daily setup is
needed while it stays powered and the normal sequence is followed.

IR DEMO MODE
For repeatable demonstrations without waiting for tomorrow, use an empty
carousel or dummy contents. Start IR test mode, clear for 1 second, hold for
1.5 seconds, remove the hand, wait for the 3-second motor pause and clear
again. Repeat as needed. Test rotations do not advance weekly calendar days
or count as daily doses. Stop the mode explicitly afterward. Starting tests
invalidates a pending READY daily record; it cannot then be retried that day.

See NORMAL_OPERATION.txt for recovery, TEST_CHECKLIST.txt for demonstration
checks and VALIDATION.txt for build/test limits. WIRING.txt is unchanged.
Motor command completion does not verify an exact pill count or ingestion.
