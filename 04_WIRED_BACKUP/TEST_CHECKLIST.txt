PHARMAVAULT WEEKLY DEMONSTRATION CHECKLIST
Use an empty mechanism or dummy contents while testing movements.

1. FIRMWARE / CONNECTION
   ESP32 115200 -> ? prints PHARMAVAULT weekly firmware 1 and dashboard URL.
   MSP430 9600 -> reset/resume prints ready (weekly 1; UART fix 1).
   Join Wi-Fi, load dashboard, Ctrl+F5; seven-day and IR test panels visible.
   Check temperature and humidity update; compare against room conditions.

2. REPEATED IR/MOTOR TEST (NO CLOCK/DAILY TIMER NEEDED)
   Verify stepper steps/direction on the empty eight-compartment mechanism.
   Start IR test mode with empty/dummy confirmation. No immediate rotation.
   Clear sensor for 1 s, then hold hand 1.5 s -> one compartment advance.
   Remove hand, wait 3 s motor cooldown and 1 s clear; repeat twice.
   Holding the hand continuously must not cause repeat movements.
   Test counter increases; daily records do not become Completed.
   Stop test mode. Actual physical alignment must be reconfirmed afterward.

3. WHOLE-WEEK SETUP
   Sync clock; save daily time shortly ahead and alert time later on the same
   date. Remove old dummy contents, park empty compartment 8; fill 1-7 with
   clearly labelled day-specific dummy packs. Tick both weekly confirmations.
   Start/refill week. No motor movement should occur at confirmation.
   Check the displayed start date: if today already has a history record,
   the new pack starts tomorrow. Do not erase history to work around this.

4. OPTIONAL NFC
   Before initial week confirmation, a card should show Load 7 days.
   During active pack, a card shows Week day N of 7 (or Week starts soon).
   Every valid kit card works without website UID assignment.
   Tapping never moves the motor, records a daily dose or resets the week.
   The weekly message lasts 8 s, then environmental LCD status returns.

5. DAILY TIME / IR DISPENSE
   Before due: hand hold does not dispense in NORMAL mode.
   At due: today becomes READY; motor still stays stationary.
   Clear sensor, hold 1.5 s -> today's compartment advances one position.
   History becomes Completed after steps finish; visually check dummy release.
   Another hand presentation that date does not dispense again.
   Do not start IR test mode mid-check: it deliberately cancels pending READY.

6. OVERDUE / MISSED DAY
   On a fresh scheduled date, wait past alert time without a hand presentation.
   Dashboard shows overdue; a late hand attempt remains possible until midnight.
   If deliberately missed, next day must target the NEXT DATE'S compartment,
   with movement blocked until missed contents are removed and its predecessor
   is manually aligned/confirmed. Alignment confirmation alone does not rotate.
   Days skipped while paused/off appear as MISSED after clock restoration.

7. DAY 7 / WEEKLY REFILL
   Day 7 command completed -> REFILL ALL 7 DAYS immediately.
   Day 7 missed -> the same reminder at midnight entering day 8.
   Seven dates count even if some were missed. No eighth medication dose.
   Card now shows Refill all 7days / Confirm in web.
   Refill/park/confirm once; verify the same saved times remain unchanged.

8. RESTART
   With no movement running, restart ESP32: motor stays off and schedule pauses.
   Saved timer, pack dates, refill latch and past attempts remain.
   Restore clock/alignment, enable. A completed date cannot run twice.
   If MSP alone restarts, readings/card lookup recover without requiring a new
   weekly refill or stopping ESP32 daily mode.

Day progression is based on the real Singapore calendar, not test rotations.
For a quick live demonstration, use repeatable IR test mode and show the normal
timer once. Do not change the laptop calendar or erase flash to simulate days
on a unit holding real medicine. The included host validation simulates seven
days, missed days, faults and restarts without physical dispensing.

Log actual observations: measured temperature/humidity, card LCD result,
stepper alignment after multiple moves, IR hold/clear behavior, USB power
stability and dummy-pill release. Software tests cannot verify these for you.
